=== Phase 4h: Year 2038 / time_t boundary analysis ===

Platform: Linux 3.10, 32-bit ARM, time_t is 32-bit.
Risk: kernel and libc syscalls will overflow in Jan 2038.
Rust mitigation: SystemTime → u64 via as_secs() is safe for scrobbler
timestamps; does NOT fix kernel RTC, filesystem timestamps, or
any Sony library that accepts/returns time_t.
